Experimental Camera

Process

This project is one in showing how we can try and change the narrative of perception and who gets to be seen within society. By using cameras and creating what is essentially a camera filter, we start to be able to comment on the issue of exclusion based on race, gender, sexuality, or any other forms of self expression that is seen as minor or subserviant to the dominant norm. In creating this distorted camera, where one cannot clearly see themselves, it makes the experience of not being wholly seen actaulized in a physical manifestation that is this camera. Here, everyone is equally unseen.
